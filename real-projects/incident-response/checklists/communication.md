## Communication Rules

1. **Use secure channels** - Do not discuss incident details over unencrypted email
2. **Use incident ID** in all communications: `INC-YYYY-NNNN`
3. **Mark all communications** as CONFIDENTIAL
4. **Do not speculate** - Only communicate confirmed facts
5. **Legal review** all external communications before sending
6. **Log all communications** in the incident report
7. **One spokesperson** for external communications (usually Legal or PR)
8. **Do not discuss** on social media or public channels
9. **BCC recipients** when sending to large groups to protect email addresses
10. **Confirm receipt** of critical notifications

## Post-Incident Communication

- [ ] Send incident closure notice to all stakeholders
- [ ] Schedule post-mortem review meeting
- [ ] Share lessons learned (internal)
- [ ] File regulatory notifications (if required)
- [ ] Send customer notifications (if required)
- [ ] Issue media statement (if required)
- [ ] Update threat intelligence community (if appropriate)
