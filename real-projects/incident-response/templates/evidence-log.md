## Chain of Custody Principles

1. **Every piece of evidence** must be documented from collection to disposal
2. **Evidence must never be left unattended** without documented handoff
3. **All transfers** must be recorded with signatures and timestamps
4. **Hash values** must be verified at every transfer point
5. **Evidence integrity** must be maintained to ensure admissibility in legal proceedings
