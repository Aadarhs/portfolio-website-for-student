# Communication Templates

## Template 1: Initial Internal Alert (IR Team Activation)

**Subject:** [URGENT] Security Incident INC-YYYY-NNNN - Activation Required

**To:** IR Team Distribution List
**Priority:** High

---

Team,

A security incident has been declared.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Incident ID** | INC-YYYY-NNNN |
| **Severity** | SEV-1 / SEV-2 / SEV-3 / SEV-4 |
| **Type** | [Ransomware / Phishing / Data Breach / DDoS / Insider / Malware] |
| **Detected** | YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM (UTC) |
| **IR Lead** | [Name] |
| **War Room** | [Physical location / Virtual meeting link] |

**Initial Summary:**
[1-2 sentence description of what was detected.]

**Immediate Actions Required:**
1. [Action 1]
2. [Action 2]
3. [Action 3]

**Next Update:** [Date/Time of next scheduled update]

Please confirm receipt and join the war room within [X] minutes.

## Template 3: Customer/External Notification

**Subject:** Security Notice - [Company Name] Security Incident

**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL - EXTERNAL DISTRIBUTION (Legal Approved)

---

Dear [Customer/Stakeholder Name],

We are writing to inform you of a security incident that may affect your information.

**What Happened:**
[Clear, factual description of the incident without technical jargon.]

**What Information Was Involved:**
[Specific types of data that may have been accessed, without speculation.]

**What We Are Doing:**
[Steps taken to address the incident and protect against future occurrences.]

**What You Can Do:**
- [Recommended action 1, e.g., change password]
- [Recommended action 2, e.g., monitor accounts]
- [Recommended action 3, e.g., place fraud alert]

**For More Information:**
[Contact information for questions, including a dedicated phone number or email if available.]

We take the protection of your information seriously and sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

Sincerely,
[Name, Title]
[Company Name]

We are conducting a thorough investigation to determine the full scope and impact. We are committed to transparency and will provide updates as our investigation progresses.

[If applicable: We have established a dedicated hotline for individuals who may have questions at [phone number].]
